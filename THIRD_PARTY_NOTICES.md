## Xenia XDVDFS research

The Xbox 360 XDVDFS image reader in `src/XdvdfsImage.cs` is an independent C#
implementation based on the documented layout and the BSD-licensed Xenia disc
image reader. Xenia copyright notices and the BSD license are retained in the
source file.

Reference:
<https://github.com/xenia-project/xenia>

## Unleashed Recompiled

The page flow and integrity-first installer concept were studied from the
GPLv3 project Unleashed Recompiled. No Unleashed Recompiled source file is
compiled into this installer. Its source remains available here:

<https://github.com/hedge-dev/UnleashedRecomp>
